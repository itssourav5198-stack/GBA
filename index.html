About CBA

Who we are

Our mission

Rules & guidelines

General rules
  • Complete the official registration and give accurate information.
  • Follow the published schedule and instructions from administrators.
  • Cheating, unfair gameplay, match manipulation and deliberate disruption are prohibited.
  • Be respectful to players and organisers. Violations can lead to warnings, disqualification or other proportionate action.
Registration
  • Register only through the official CBA system. Confirmation comes through official channels.
  • Team games may need complete team details.
  • Incorrect or duplicate registrations may be rejected after verification.
  • Fees, if any, are shown before payment. Registration does not guarantee participation until confirmed.
Fair play
  • No unauthorised cheats or hacks, and no bug exploitation for advantage.
  • No match fixing, impersonation or unauthorised account sharing.
  • No harassment, abusive language, threats or interference with operations.
  • Organisers may investigate reports and let participants respond where appropriate.
Match rules and disqualification

Game-specific rulebooks cover match format, team size, duration, scoring, tie-breakers, qualification, disputes and disqualification. The game rulebook takes precedence over these general rules.

Serious or repeated violations (cheating, impersonation, fraudulent registration, match manipulation) can lead to disqualification, based on reasonable evidence and the published rules.
